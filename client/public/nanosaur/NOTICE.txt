What is hosted here
-------------------
Nanosaur.js, Nanosaur.wasm and Nanosaur.data are the unmodified build artifacts of the WebAssembly port's
"Build WebAssembly" / "Deploy to GitHub Pages" workflow for master commit
42c543f (https://github.com/LachlanBWWright/Nanosaur-android/tree/42c543f), as published at
https://lachlanbwwright.github.io/Nanosaur-android/game/ (downloaded 2026-11-16; the workflow's own
artifact had expired). Nanosaur.data is byte-identical to the gh-pages branch build (5fa0ce5).

index.html, play.js, play.css and webgl-fixes.js (the page around the game: loader, touch buttons,
credits, and a small WebGL compatibility layer that converts texture formats and index sizes the
browser can't take) were written for this site. The game files themselves are not modified.
